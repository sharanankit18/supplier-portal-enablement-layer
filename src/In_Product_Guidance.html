How to add a manufacturing site

Before you start: operating license number, food-safety certificate, and site address.
  1. Go to Sites → Add manufacturing site.
  2. Enter site details and confirm the licensed address.
  3. Upload your food-safety certificate.
  4. Submit — track status from the Sites dashboard.
